Terms used around the wiki, in alphabetical order. This glossary is a starting point and grows along with the sections. If anything is unclear, start with <EntityLink to="guide:how-to-use" />.

## Terms

<dl>
  <dt>Boss</dt>
  <dd>A monster stronger than the rest, which shows up at places and times of its own.</dd>
  <dt>Drop</dt>
  <dd>An item a monster leaves when defeated. The wiki shows how often each one drops by tier, never by percentage.</dd>
  <dt>Drop tier</dt>
  <dd>Always, common, uncommon, rare or very rare: the wiki's way of saying how often an item drops.</dd>
  <dt>Spawn</dt>
  <dd>The spot on the map where a monster appears, and appears again after a while.</dd>
  <dt>Twin page</dt>
  <dd>The same page in another language. Every page on the wiki has them.</dd>
</dl>
