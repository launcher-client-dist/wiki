The Opengea wiki gathers what the community knows about the game in one place. This guide shows how to find things and how to read each page.

## Search

Search sits at the top of every page. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>/</kbd>) from anywhere, type part of a name and pick with the arrow keys. Search looks in the language of the page you are on, and results are grouped by kind: monsters, items, guides…

<Callout type="tip">
  Can't remember the exact name? Type just a piece of it. Search understands partial words and ignores accents.
</Callout>

## Two languages

Every page exists in Portuguese and in English. The **PT / EN** switch at the top takes you to the same page in the other language, and names show in both languages, so you recognise what you see in the game.

## Links with a preview

Hover over (or tap) a highlighted name to see a quick card without leaving the page. Try it with the <EntityLink to="guide:glossary" />. While a section hasn't opened yet, the name shows without a link, but its quick card works just the same.

## How often an item drops

The wiki never shows drop percentages. Each item has a tier, with a colour, a name and little bars:

- <TierBadge tier="sempre" /> drops on almost every kill
- <TierBadge tier="comum" /> drops often
- <TierBadge tier="incomum" /> drops now and then
- <TierBadge tier="raro" /> drops seldom
- <TierBadge tier="muito-raro" /> drops very rarely

## Light or dark

The sun or moon button at the top switches the theme. The wiki remembers your choice; if you never chose, it follows your device's theme.
